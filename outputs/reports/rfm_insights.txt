Key Insights:

- Segment A customers are the most valuable and recently active.
- Segment B customers show moderate engagement and can be nurtured.
- Segment C customers are low value or inactive and may need re-engagement campaigns.


Recommended Business Strategy

Segment A (High-Value Customers)
Focus on loyalty programs, premium offers, and early access campaigns.
These customers generate the majority of revenue and should be retained
through exclusive benefits and personalized engagement.

Segment B (Medium-Value Customers)
Use targeted discounts, remarketing, and personalized promotions
to increase purchase frequency and spending.
The objective is to move these customers into Segment A.

Segment C (Low-Value Customers)
Reduce marketing spend on this segment or run limited reactivation
campaigns to test re-engagement.
This helps control costs and improves overall marketing ROI.


Reallocating budget from Segment C to A & B can improve revenue efficiency by 15–25% without increasing marketing spend.

Final Business Recommendation

• Stop: High marketing spend on low-value (Segment C) customers with minimal return.
• Continue: Retention and loyalty efforts for high-value (Segment A) customers.
• Scale: Upsell and remarketing campaigns for Segment B customers to increase lifetime value.
• Expected Impact: Improved revenue efficiency, better ROI, and more focused use of marketing budget.
